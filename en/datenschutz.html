2. Summary

UnitPair calculates exclusively on your device. There is no account and no server to which the app transmits anything. The numbers you enter for converting are neither stored nor transmitted; only the app's settings remain on the device, and the app does not create an automatic backup in a cloud.

The free version uses Google AdMob. In the process, technical data and device identifiers may be transmitted to Google and its advertising partners; which processing takes place depends in particular on your consent decision. With Premium, ads are no longer loaded; the app also no longer asks for consent.

On iOS, the operating system additionally asks whether the app may use your advertising identifier (App Tracking Transparency). Without this permission, the identifier is not transmitted.

3. Data that stays on your device

The app stores only its settings on your device: your home country, the selected destination country and the destination countries you chose recently, language and display, and whether you have bought Premium. It does not store the numbers you enter; they are gone when you close the app.

To suggest a home country on first launch, the app reads the region from your device's language and region settings. This happens only on the device. The app does not request your location.

This data is not transmitted to us or to third parties. We have no access to it. The app does not create an automatic backup in a cloud either: It is configured so that its settings are excluded from automatic Android backup (allowBackup="false" together with exclusion rules, also for transfer to a new device); on iOS, the folder containing the settings is excluded from iCloud backup. Whatever the operating system and device manufacturer back up beyond that is outside our control. On a new device, you therefore choose your home country again. Uninstalling the app deletes the settings from the device.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob) and consent

In the free version, the app embeds advertising from Google AdMob. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland.

In doing so, Google may process in particular your IP address and an approximate location derived from it, device and advertising identifiers, information about app launches and interactions with the app and with ads, as well as diagnostic and performance data. Which identifiers are available and what they may be used for depends on your consent and your system settings. Google states that the purposes include advertising, analytics and fraud prevention.

Besides Google, advertising partners are involved. If you consent, third-party advertising companies may also process this data and store or read information on your device, for example for personalized advertising, measurement of ad performance and audience research. Who these are in detail is shown in the consent dialog under “List of partners”; the list there is always current, because it can change. Via “Manage options” in the same dialog, you can consent or object to individual purposes and providers separately.

Before the first ad request, the app obtains your decision through Google's consent platform (UMP); until it is available, no ad is requested. Which ads and which data processing are permitted depends on your decision: without the required consent for storing or reading information on your device, it may be that only limited ads (“Limited Ads”) can be delivered. The app remains fully usable in any case.

Insofar as consent is required for the use of AdMob, the storing or reading of information on your device is based on Section 25(1) of the German Telecommunications Digital Services Data Protection Act (TDDDG), and the associated processing of personal data on Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. Further processing by Google and the advertising partners is governed by their own statements on purposes and legal bases; some providers rely on a legitimate interest, which you can object to in the dialog under “Manage options”.

If you decline, limited ads may still be requested where applicable. For this, too, your IP address is transmitted to Google, because an ad cannot otherwise be delivered. The legal basis in this respect is Section 25(2) no. 2 TDDDG and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, that is, our interest in funding the free version through advertising.

Processing may also take place outside the European Economic Area, in particular in the USA, both by Google and by the advertising partners. For transfers to Google LLC, Google relies on the European Commission's adequacy decision on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (Art. 45 GDPR), under which Google LLC is certified, and otherwise on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses (Art. 46 GDPR). Further details, including how to view the clauses, at https://policies.google.com/privacy/frameworks. Which safeguards the individual advertising partners rely on is stated in their entries in the partner list of the consent dialog.

Retention period: Neither we nor the app store the data processed through AdMob on your device. How long Google and the advertising partners keep it depends on the type of data and the purpose and is stated in their privacy notices; Google states, for instance, that it anonymizes advertising log data after fixed periods. Details are in the Google notices linked below and in the partners' entries in the consent dialog.

Additionally on iOS: App Tracking Transparency

On iOS, Apple asks through a system dialog whether UnitPair may use your advertising identifier (IDFA) to track your activity across apps and websites owned by other companies. Apple's dialog shows a short explanation from the app: “Allows the advertising identifier to be used for ads that suit you and to measure them. This is how the free version pays for itself.” Outside Europe, an explanation screen from Google may appear beforehand; in Europe, the consent dialog takes its place. iOS generally shows the dialog once per installation, after the GDPR consent, provided your system settings allow the request. The two decisions are independent of each other.

Without “Allow”, the app gets no access to the advertising identifier (IDFA), and Google may not track you across apps and websites of other companies. Which ads you then see and which other identifiers Google may use depends on your choices in the consent dialog. The app remains fully usable; you suffer no disadvantages.

To measure the success of ads, Google may use Apple's SKAdNetwork mechanism. No identifier of your device is transmitted in the process.

With Premium, no advertising is loaded. The dialog does not appear there.

You can change your decision at any time: in the Settings tab under “Privacy options”. You change the tracking permission on iOS in the iOS Settings under Privacy & Security → Tracking, at the switch for UnitPair.

Further information: https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

9. Your rights

Subject to the statutory requirements, you have in particular the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing and data portability. Insofar as we process personal data on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you have the right to object to this processing at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR). You object to processing by Google and the advertising partners based on legitimate interests in the consent dialog under “Manage options”. You can withdraw consent you have given at any time with effect for the future: for the advertising consent via “Privacy options” in the Settings tab, for the tracking permission on iOS via the iOS Settings under Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Provision of data: You do not have to give us any personal data to use the app; there is no account and no registration. Consent to advertising and, on iOS, to tracking is voluntary. If you decline, the app remains fully usable. Only for the purchase of Premium do you have to provide the respective store with the data needed to process the purchase; without it, the purchase cannot be completed. Contacting us by email is voluntary as well.

To exercise your rights, you can contact us at any time (contact details above). The app's settings are stored on your device; we have no access to them, and the advertising data processed by Google is not held by us. Insofar as Google or an advertising partner is independently responsible for a processing operation, you can additionally assert your rights directly with them. The providers and their contact channels are listed in the consent dialog under “List of partners”.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular with our competent supervisory authority, the State Commissioner for Data Protection of Lower Saxony (Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz Niedersachsen), or with the supervisory authority at your place of residence, your place of work or the place of the alleged infringement.
